# Illustration de la vie sur la ligne

Retouche produite avec l’outil d’image intégré à partir du logo utilisé comme référence de style.
Cette exploration est conservée à titre historique ; la fiche utilise désormais les
personnages extraits du logo officiel.

Use case: logo-brand. Generate a NEW small horizontal decorative illustration for the bottom edge of an A5 community checklist, using the attached coral hand-drawn carrot logo solely as a strict STYLE reference. Output a truly transparent PNG. Make one long very slightly wobbly ground line, and 4 tiny people that LIVE ON that line, in the same naive and human hand-drawn stick-figure manner as the attached hand-drawn logo, with slightly wobbly thick wax crayon/soft marker strokes, irregular edges, imperfect round heads and loose natural lively poses. One child runs at left, one is bent over planting a tiny carrot in the earth with a small bit of root below the line, and at right is a small simple freestanding swing built from four crooked toothpick posts, with one small child on the swing and another beside it. Leave lots of clear space between figures. Simple readable thumbnail silhouettes. The ground line runs uninterrupted nearly from left edge to right edge, with all characters' feet and swing legs placed directly on it. The whole artwork is a very wide shallow landscape horizontal strip, about 5 times wider than tall, with surrounding transparent space. Single coral pink ink #df8278 only, true transparent background, no paper, no gradients, no fill blocks, no text, no logos, no hand-lettering, no borders, no additional decoration. Absolutely no rigid geometric vector clip art, smooth perfect circles, childlike cartoon faces, photorealism or complex detail. A simple, friendly human handmade wax-crayon drawing matching the reference's actual stroke and figure style.
